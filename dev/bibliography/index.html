Bibliography

Methods

  • H. M. Markowitz, The Elimination Form of the Inverse and its Application to Linear Programming, Management Science 3(3) (1957), 255–269, doi:10.1287/mnsc.3.3.255.
    The Markowitz score (r − 1)(c − 1) that orders the search of the unit pivots.

  • B. A. LaMacchia, A. M. Odlyzko, Solving Large Sparse Linear Systems Over Finite Fields, in A. J. Menezes, S. A. Vanstone (eds.), Advances in Cryptology – CRYPTO '90, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 537, Springer (1991), 109–133, doi:10.1007/3-540-38424-3_8.
    Structured elimination, here of the rows and columns of degree one with a unit entry, before and between the Markowitz pivots.

  • J.-G. Dumas, B. D. Saunders, G. Villard, On Efficient Sparse Integer Matrix Smith Normal Form Computations, Journal of Symbolic Computation 32(1–2) (2001), 71–99, doi:10.1006/jsco.2001.0451.
    Smith normal form of large sparse integer matrices, in particular from homology.

  • J.-G. Dumas, F. Heckenbach, B. D. Saunders, V. Welker, Computing Simplicial Homology Based on Efficient Smith Normal Form Algorithms, in M. Joswig, N. Takayama (eds.), Algebra, Geometry and Software Systems, Springer (2003), 177–206, doi:10.1007/978-3-662-05148-1_10.
    Review and comparison of Smith normal form algorithms on sparse boundary matrices.

Software

  • C. Fieker, W. Hart, T. Hofmann, F. Johansson, Nemo/Hecke: Computer Algebra and Number Theory Packages for the Julia Programming Language, in Proceedings of ISSAC '17, ACM (2017), 157–164, doi:10.1145/3087604.3087611.
    Hecke computes the elementary divisors of the core.

  • The FLINT team, FLINT: Fast Library for Number Theory (2025), flintlib.org.
    The integer arithmetic of the core, through Nemo.

  • W. Bosma, J. Cannon, C. Playoust, The Magma Algebra System I: The User Language, Journal of Symbolic Computation 24(3–4) (1997), 235–265, doi:10.1006/jsco.1996.0125.
    The computer algebra system whose performance the package aims at.

File format

  • R. F. Boisvert, R. Pozo, K. A. Remington, The Matrix Market Exchange Formats: Initial Design, NIST IR 5935, National Institute of Standards and Technology (1996), doi:10.6028/NIST.IR.5935.
    The format of read_matrix_market and write_matrix_market.

Matrices of the benchmarks

  • P. Dehornoy, Y. Lafont, Homology of Gaussian Groups, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 53(2) (2003), 489–540, doi:10.5802/aif.1951.
    The resolution whose reduced version gives the G₃₇ matrices.

  • N. Idrissi, V. Roca i Lucio, Homology of configuration spaces in positive characteristic via point-set constructions, arXiv:2606.26802.
    The ZT2w4m5 matrices come from a construction in this article.
